# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link is the code of conduct for every project space:
issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel representing Mangue.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Reporting

Report instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to
the maintainer at **rntgspr@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer applies the
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent
ban, according to the impact of the behavior.
